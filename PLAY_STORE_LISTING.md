# Unitify Play Store Listing Draft

## App name

Unitify – All-in-One Converter

## Short description

Fast, accurate offline conversions for everyday units—all in one place.

## Full description

Convert everyday measurements quickly and confidently with Unitify, a polished all-in-one converter that works completely offline.

Choose from 14 useful categories: length, weight and mass, temperature, area, volume, speed, time, data storage, pressure, energy, power, frequency, angle, and fuel consumption. Results update instantly as you type, with support for accurate temperature offsets, decimal and binary storage units, and US and imperial fuel-economy formulas.

Highlights:

- Fast, accurate conversions with no Convert button
- 14 categories and more than 90 units
- Smart search by category, unit name, or symbol
- Favorite unit pairs for one-tap access
- Recent conversion history stored only on your device
- Quick unit swapping and result copying
- Configurable decimal precision
- Light, dark, and system themes
- Fully offline—no account, ads, analytics, or tracking
- Accessible Material 3 interface designed for different screen sizes

Your conversion preferences, favorites, and history remain on your device and can be cleared at any time from Settings.
